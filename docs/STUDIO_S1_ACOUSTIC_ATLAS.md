## Architectural decisions: one acoustic scene, separate identity and coordinate spaces

1. Reference catalog: portable plate ID plus stable marker ID, immutable source document. Keep the 168 existing VSL seating IDs, 118 atlas role IDs and 559 M45 instrument IDs distinct; any crosswalk is explicit and reviewed. No identity matching by display name.
2. Project binding: explicit association of a real Tracktion EditItemID to one selected reference marker, or a reviewed collection of markers representing an ensemble patch. Unassigned markers and existing tracks remain visible. Preserve existing routing choices; no automatic track creation, plug-in load, gain change or enablement. Track deletion invalidates its binding, never redirects it.
3. Schematic chart coordinates: normalized x rightward from conductor viewpoint and y downstage; legacy Swift semicircle coordinates and existing StagePan coordinates are different. Preserve raw atlas x/y and original arc. A review preview may use normalized chart x = 2*x-1 and chart depth = 1-y, but these values are not automatically Stage pan, nor physical distances. Existing STUDIO_STAGE_SEAT remains authoritative until user explicitly applies placement. Source schematic must be calibrated to a stage footprint before metres are inferred.
4. Physical scene: metres in a room coordinate system, origin at center front of playing area, +X conductor right, +Y upstage, +Z up. Stage width/depth, room dimensions, stage origin and listener/microphone positions are independent. Source heights and spread are explicitly authored, not inferred from diagram sizes. Legacy data keeps existing stereo balance and depth send unless user enables an upgraded scene. No silent migration.
5. Existing rooms and routing: retain wet-only eight-line modulated FDN, six-band decay targets, 12 shared early taps and 0–100 ms predelay, solo-safe return, Room Check, project presets, and current dry mix. Keep Normal Mix as default. New spatial processing must be opt-in, with bypass comparison and safe undo.

## Target audio graph (S2–S8, not implemented here)

Instrument output -> source-stage transform (direct level/delay, spread and optional directivity) -> normal stereo mix OR explicit virtual-mic monitoring. Bounded first-order source/receiver image reflections use the room surfaces and source/receiver position. A shared stereo/diffuse late field stays one FDN per room, not one reverb per instrument or per capsule. In upgraded mode avoid double-counting legacy shared early taps; preserve old room sound in Legacy mode. Mic pickup has signed first-order directional response where relevant (figure eight retains polarity); delays come from distance / speed of sound with causal compensation; maintain a distinct manual delay trim. Multi-mic mix is not added on top of normal dry mix unless explicitly chosen as a blend. Stereo library patches require an explicit representation (preserve stereo spread, or user-selected mono point); do not collapse all patches automatically or imply removal of baked-in ambience. Meter direct, early and late components separately. Provide pre/post-fader routing policy and prevent double counting at group bus; room return cannot send to itself.
